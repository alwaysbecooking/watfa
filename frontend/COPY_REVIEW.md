## 7. FAQ

### Is this an app?

**Current**

> No. WATFA is a community-sourced open public dataset—not an app, platform, startup or product. It may use forms, bots, a website or an app as its interface, but those can change. What matters is the shared dataset and the process for maintaining it.

**Suggested**

> No. You may access WATFA through forms, bots, a website, or an app, but WATFA itself is a community-sourced open public dataset—not an app, platform, startup, or product. The interface may change. What matters is the shared dataset and the process that keeps it useful.

### Animal privacy

**Current question**

> What about animal privacy?

**Suggested question**

> How can you protect animal privacy?

**Current answer**

> Precise locations help research and identification, but can also help thieves, smugglers or people trying to harm or illegally relocate animals. Public locations should stay **deliberately fuzzy** and evolve as we learn what works.

**Suggested answer**

> Precise locations can help you identify animals and support research, but they can also expose animals to thieves, smugglers, or people trying to harm or illegally relocate them. Keep public locations **deliberately fuzzy**, and adjust what you share as the community learns what works.

### Animal rescue

**Current question**

> Can WATFA help with animal rescue?

**Suggested question**

> Can WATFA help you rescue an animal?

**Current answer**

> Rescue is not WATFA's primary focus. If an animal is injured or in distress, use a dedicated service such as [Karuna Nidhan](https://karunanidhan.app/). We would rather integrate with existing rescue networks than replace or interfere with them.

**Suggested answer**

> Rescue is not WATFA’s primary focus. If you find an animal that is injured or in distress, use a dedicated service such as [Karuna Nidhan](https://karunanidhan.app/). WATFA is designed to work with existing rescue networks rather than replace or interfere with them.

The section label `05`, heading `FAQ`, and link text `Karuna Nidhan` remain unchanged.
